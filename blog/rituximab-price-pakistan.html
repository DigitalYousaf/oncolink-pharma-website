Rituximab price in Pakistan: brands and availability

12 Nov 2026 · Oncolink Pharma Distributors

Rituximab is prescribed for several blood cancers and other conditions. Prices vary a lot by brand. Here is what it costs in Pakistan and how to get it.

What does rituximab cost in Pakistan?

Rituximab comes in several brands. At Oncolink, the current prices are:

  • Ristova vial (rituximab): Rs 20,000 to Rs 100,000, depending on strength
  • Mabthera vial 1400 mg: Rs 120,000
  • RITUXIM injection: Rs 22,000 to Rs 44,000
  • Zytux injection: Rs 11,000 to Rs 36,000

Prices can change with the supplier, so always confirm today's price on WhatsApp before you order.

Why do prices differ between brands?

Different companies make rituximab under different brand names. The original brand usually costs more. Local and biosimilar brands cost less. Your doctor prescribes rituximab, and the brand can be discussed with your doctor and pharmacist.

Is rituximab a cold chain medicine?

Yes. Rituximab must be kept at 2 to 8 C. At Oncolink it stays in our cold room, then travels in insulated packaging with cold packs. When it arrives, put it in your fridge straight away.

How to order in Lahore

Search for rituximab on this website, pick the brand your doctor prescribed, and tap Order on WhatsApp. We confirm the price and availability, then deliver the same day in Lahore, or across Pakistan.
