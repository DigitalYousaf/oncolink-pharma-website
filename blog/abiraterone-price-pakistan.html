Abiraterone price in Pakistan and where to buy it

12 Nov 2026 · Oncolink Pharma Distributors

Abiraterone is a medicine used in prostate cancer treatment. If your doctor has prescribed it, the first question is usually the price. Here is what it costs in Pakistan, what affects the price, and how to buy it safely.

What does abiraterone cost in Pakistan?

Abiraterone comes in different brands and strengths. At Oncolink, the current prices are:

  • Abiteron 250 mg: Rs 199,788
  • ABYTIGA (abiraterone acetate): Rs 26,000 to Rs 145,400, depending on strength and pack
  • ABIRA-AHP 250 mg: Rs 30,000

Prices can change with the supplier, so always confirm today's price on WhatsApp before you order.

Why does the price vary so much?

Three things decide the price. First, the brand. Original brands cost more than local brands. Second, the strength and the pack size. Third, availability. When a brand is short in the market, the price goes up.

Your doctor prescribes the generic medicine, abiraterone. The brand can be discussed with your doctor and pharmacist, because the price difference is large.
