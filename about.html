About Oncolink Pharma Distributors

Supplying genuine medicines across Pakistan since 2006.

Oncolink Pharma Distributors is a trusted pharmaceutical company committed to supplying high-quality medicines across Pakistan. We specialize in oncology and specialty care, ensuring timely delivery, proper storage, and genuine products. Our focus is reliability, compliance, and customer satisfaction, supporting healthcare providers with efficient and professional services every day.

Our story

Oncolink Pharma Distributor was established in 2006. Since then we have served patients as well as institutions, and we offer distribution services to multinational and national pharmaceutical companies in Lahore.

Our objective is simple. Provide the best service in the supply of quality medicines to customers, patients, doctors, pharmacies and government institutions, on top priority, to save human lives.

How we work

Our vision is to provide the best services to companies and promote their business. We have qualified, trained and experienced professional staff working as per Oncolink SOPs.

We have established Standard Operating Procedures in compliance with WHO Good Distribution Practices. We ensure that only the best quality product reaches our clients. A dedicated team of highly trained professionals examines each product before dispatch.

We cover government institutions, private institutions, pharmacies and patients in Lahore, Gujranwala, Faisalabad, Multan and other allocated areas of Pakistan.

What we stand for

  • Commitment. We do what we say we will do.
  • On time delivery. Same day delivery in Lahore for urgent orders.
  • Special care of our customers. Patients, doctors, pharmacies and hospitals all get the same attention.
  • Special care of our suppliers. We work closely with the companies whose products we distribute.
  • Our cold chain system. Temperature sensitive medicines stay at 2 to 8 C.
  • Quality of products and services. Checked before dispatch, every time.
  • Coverage of area. Lahore and beyond, across Pakistan.
  • Team work. Trained staff, clear procedures, no shortcuts.
