Cold chain, done properly

Some medicines only work if they stay cold. We keep them at 2 to 8 C, from our cold room to your door.

Cancer medicines are sensitive. If they get warm, they can lose their effect. That is why cold chain is not a nice extra for us. It is the core of what we do.

How we keep medicines cold

  • Cold room storage. Temperature sensitive medicines are stored at 2 to 8 C in our cold room.
  • Three power sources. Solar system is our primary source of electricity. Wapda is the second source. A generator is the third. The cold room keeps running even when the power goes out.
  • Yearly calibration. Calibration and validation is done each year for all refrigerators, so the temperature you see is the temperature you get.
  • Insulated packaging. All temperature sensitive shipments travel in thermostable boxes with cold packs, so the medicine stays cold on the road.
  • Checked before dispatch. A dedicated team examines each product before it leaves us.

What this means for you

If your medicine needs cold storage, tell us when you order. We pack it in insulated packaging with cold packs and deliver it the same day in Lahore. When it arrives, put it in your fridge straight away, at 2 to 8 C, and follow your doctor's storage advice.
